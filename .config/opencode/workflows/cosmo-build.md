# Build the prepared plan from a handoff

Each of the following phases must be completed in sequence and in full before moving to the next phase.


## Phase 1

Identify the plan, read the messages in the sessions backwards until you find a handoff - this is the only plan to build. Note its id.

Failing to find any plan aborts the build.


## Phase 2


Preform the tasks laid out in the plan, no more, no less. You are allowed to correct warnings and errors from formatting and linting.

Needing to write outside the scope of the plan to complete the plan aborts the build.


## Phase 3

Report briefly what you changed and how you verified it.
